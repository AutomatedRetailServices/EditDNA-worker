# CutSell Privacy Policy

DRAFT for review — last updated 15 October 2026.

CutSell ("we", "us") is an app that turns the videos you choose into short, edited cuts. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Videos, photos and audio you choose.** The videos you upload to make a cut, and the photos, videos, voice-overs and music you add in the editor.
- **What your video says.** A text transcript of the speech in your videos, made so we can find your best takes and write captions.
- **Your edits.** The cuts, captions, overlays, sounds and settings you make in the editor.
- **Account details.** A random account ID. If you sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple gives us and, if you share it, your email address.
- **Basic technical information.** The model of your phone and the size and format of your videos, used to process them correctly and fix problems.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## How we use it

We use your data only to run CutSell:
- to make your cuts;
- to show your videos and edits in the app;
- to export your finished video;
- to keep your account working;
- to fix problems.

## Services that process your data for us

To make a cut, CutSell sends your content to these providers:

- **Deepgram:** speech to text. It receives the audio of your videos.
- **Anthropic (Claude):** AI that helps choose the best takes. It receives the transcript of your videos, not the video itself.
- **Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3):** stores your videos, media and finished exports.
- **Modal:** the computers that process and render your videos.
- **Render:** hosts our servers.

We share your data with these providers only so they can provide their service to us. Each processes it under its own terms for business customers.

You are asked to agree to this before your first cut is processed. Nothing is processed until you agree.

## How long we keep it

We keep your videos, edits and exports while your account exists, so you can come back to your cuts. You can delete a cut at any time. When you delete your account, we delete your cuts, the media you uploaded and your exports from our servers.

## Delete your account

In the app, go to **You → Delete Account**. This deletes your account and everything in it. It cannot be undone.

## Children

CutSell is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
